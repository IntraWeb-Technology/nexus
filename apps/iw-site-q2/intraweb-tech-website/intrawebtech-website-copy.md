## COPY RULES (for ongoing use)

1. Never say "web design agency" — say "AI and automation studio" or "engineering practice"
2. Lead with outcomes, not tools — "automate your lead intake" not "we use n8n"
3. Use real numbers wherever possible — hours saved, cost, timeline
4. No em dashes in published copy
5. No buzzwords: "robust," "cutting-edge," "innovative," "seamless," "leverage" (the noun), "synergy"
6. OK to say: "we build," "we engineer," "we integrate," "we automate," "we diagnose"
7. CTAs should create low friction — diagnostic is the lowest-commitment entry point, push it
8. Pricing should be visible — it qualifies leads and signals professionalism
9. Voice is direct, honest, engineering-first — not corporate, not hustle-culture
10. Always position the Diagnostic as the recommended starting point for unknown-fit leads
