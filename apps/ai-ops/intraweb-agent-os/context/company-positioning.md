# Company Positioning

**Authority:** This file consumes IntraWeb positioning. It does **not** redefine it.
Canonical source: `apps/iw-site-q2/docs/doctrine/01-positioning-foundation.md` (Positioning Foundation v2).

## Company

IntraWeb Technology is a senior-led software engineering company that builds, integrates, modernizes, and stabilizes production systems.

Supporting territory: We engineer production software and the systems around it.

## Core Positioning

Engineering delivery is the organizing identity. Clients engage IntraWeb when experienced software engineering is needed for a real product, system, application, integration, automation, or operational problem.

Primary capability territories (overlapping; not a service catalog):

1. Product Engineering
2. Systems Integration
3. Modernization & Rescue
4. Automation & AI
5. Production Reliability

Operational infrastructure remains legitimate expertise as a problem domain. It is not the exclusive company category.

## We Are Not Positioned As

- A generic web-design agency
- A marketing agency
- A no-code automation shop
- A staff-augmentation body shop
- A SaaS product
- An AI consultancy selling AI adoption as an end in itself
- A strategy consultancy that stops at recommendations
- A collection of disconnected technical services

This does not prohibit overlapping work. Engineering delivery remains the identity.

## We Are

A senior-led engineering delivery company focused on production responsibility, including reducing:

- product delivery friction
- fragile or dangerous-to-change systems
- brittle integrations and conflicting data
- workflow friction and coordination drag
- founder / institutional-memory dependency
- production instability and release risk

## Core Philosophy

Engineering consequence matters more than technical demonstration or capability theater.

Do not say:

> Look what we automated.

Prefer:

> Look what stopped depending on manual coordination — or what became safe to change in production.

"We build" is legitimate when it names meaningful engineering responsibility. Generic "we build solutions" theater is not.

## Language Rules

Use restrained, engineering-plain language.

Avoid:

- AI-powered (as identity lead)
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- next-generation
- digital transformation
- solutions (as empty noun)
- leverage
- startup hype
- agency language
- consultant theater
- unsupported metrics / floating statistics

Prefer:

- production systems
- engineering responsibility
- integration / modernization / rescue (when accurate)
- dependency removal
- system continuity
- workflow reliability
- implementation discipline
- observable process movement
- operational / production resilience

## Cross-Property Boundary

Schibelli.com answers who John Schibelli is as an engineer.
IntraWeb answers what engineering work the company can take responsibility for delivering.
Shared facts may cross. Positioning authority may not. Stop and ask on cross-property decisions.
