# Company Positioning

## Company
IntraWeb Technologies is an AI-native operational systems company.

## Core Positioning
IntraWeb builds operational infrastructure that connects how a company works to how it grows.

## We Are Not
- A traditional web agency
- A freelance dev shop
- A generic AI consultancy
- An automation vendor
- A creative agency selling visuals

## We Are
An operational systems firm focused on reducing:
- workflow friction
- coordination drag
- founder dependency
- tribal knowledge
- disconnected systems
- operational fragility

## Core Philosophy
Operational consequence matters more than technical demonstration.

Do not say:
> Look what we automated.

Say:
> Look what stopped depending on manual coordination.

## Language Rules
Use restrained, operational language.

Avoid:
- AI-powered
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- next-generation
- digital transformation
- solutions
- leverage
- startup hype
- agency language
- consultant theater

Prefer:
- operational infrastructure
- dependency removal
- system continuity
- workflow reliability
- implementation discipline
- observable process movement
- operational resilience
