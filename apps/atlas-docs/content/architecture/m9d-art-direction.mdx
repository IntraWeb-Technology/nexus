## Accessibility decisions

- Mobile menu: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, Escape closes, focus moves into panel then restores to trigger, backdrop closes, body scroll locked while open
- Footer socials: icon-only with `aria-label`
- Pagination: `aria-current="page"` on current page control
- Archive / featured: single interactive target per card (no nested Read links)
- Primary buttons expose visible focus-visible outlines
